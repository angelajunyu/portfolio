ABOUT

Jen Qian is a photographer and visual artist drawn to the everyday rituals that make an image feel lived in.

Working across food, place, and portraiture, she looks for texture, gesture, and the quiet chemistry between people and their surroundings. Her works are built around atmosphere, familiarity, and a point of view that lingers.

Available for fashion, portrait, food, and editorial collaborations.
